Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Scarflight ("we", "us", or "our") develops and maintains the Scarflight application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

Scarflight is designed to help users connect to compatible flight controllers, view configuration settings, and manage supported drone configuration workflows.

Depending on the features you use and how the application is configured, the Service may process the following information:

  • Device Information: Basic operating system, application version, and device information needed for compatibility and troubleshooting.
  • Flight Controller Information: Configuration parameters, firmware information, sensor readings, and other data requested from a connected flight controller.
  • Connection Information: Connection status, device identifiers, and local network addresses when required for supported connections.
  • Diagnostic Information: Error messages and technical logs, if diagnostic logging is enabled.

We do not intentionally collect personal information unless it is necessary for a feature you use or you voluntarily provide it.

2. Local Processing and Storage

Scarflight may process flight controller data locally on your computer or connected device to provide its configuration features.

Data may be stored locally when required by application functionality, such as saving user preferences or configuration backups. You should review saved configuration files before sharing them, as they may contain device-specific information.

Unless explicitly implemented and disclosed in the application, we do not claim to upload your flight controller configurations to our servers.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by the Service may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain application functionality.
  • Connect to supported flight controllers and devices.
  • Display configuration parameters and device status.
  • Save preferences and support configuration backups.
  • Diagnose errors and improve application stability.
  • Protect the Service against misuse and technical issues.

4. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

Some features may require an internet connection to download firmware, application updates, documentation, or other resources. Such features may communicate with third-party services.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the independent privacy practices of services that we do not operate.

If analytics, crash reporting, cloud synchronization, or other third-party integrations are added to the application, this policy should be updated to identify those services and explain the information they collect.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The desktop application does not necessarily use browser cookies. If the Service includes a website or embedded web content, cookies or similar technologies may be used where necessary for functionality, preferences, or analytics.

We do not claim to use advertising trackers unless such functionality is actually implemented.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information to third parties. Information may be disclosed only when reasonably necessary:

  • To provide a feature explicitly requested by the user.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.
  • To protect the rights, security, and integrity of the Service.
  • To work with service providers that support the Service, where applicable and subject to appropriate safeguards.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you delete it or uninstall the application. Uninstalling the application may not automatically delete every configuration backup, log, or preference file.

If a future feature stores information on a server, its retention and deletion procedures should be described here.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the Service. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Users should keep their devices secure, protect configuration backups, and avoid sharing sensitive connection details.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through features designed to collect such data. If you believe personal information has been collected inappropriately, please contact us.
